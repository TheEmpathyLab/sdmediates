Privacy

How I handle what you send

Last updated October 8, 2026

Reaching out about a hard situation takes trust. Here's plainly what happens to the information you share through this site.

What I collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, the topic you pick, and your message. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers.

Where it goes

  • Formspree receives the form and emails it to me. It keeps a copy of submissions in my account.
  • Google Workspace hosts my email, so your message and our replies are stored there.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces. Like any site you load, Google sees your IP address when it sends them.

That's the whole list. I don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

How I use it

To reply to you and set up an intro call. You'll get one automatic confirmation email from hello@sdmediates.com, then a personal reply from me.

How long I keep it

If we don't end up working together, I delete your form submission and our emails on request, or within a year if you don't ask. If we do work together, what's kept and how it's kept confidential is set out in the agreement to mediate we both sign before we begin.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete anything you've sent. Email hello@sdmediates.com and I'll take care of it within a week.

Questions

Write to hello@sdmediates.com or call (678) 310-8503.
